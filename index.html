User Manual

Daily Boss & HP Bar

Each day you face a boss with 100% HP. Completing General Daily Tasks chips away the first 75% (Basic HP). The final 25% (Special HP) can only be cleared by winning the Final Slay Trial quiz. The HP bar and card glow red ("enraged") once the boss is below 25% HP.

General Daily Tasks

Add tasks with a category and tap + Add. Tap the checkbox — or swipe a task right — to mark it done and earn its XP. Repeat Yesterday restores any tasks from your last session that you've since removed. Reset Tasks manually clears today's checkmarks (this also happens automatically each new day).

Streaks

Finish every task before the day rolls over and your streak (shown as a 🔥 badge on the Boss HUD) goes up by one. Missing even one task resets it to zero the next day.

Practice Drills

Log solved problems per subject with +1 or the quicker +5 button — each problem is worth +10 Weekly XP. Use - to correct a mistaken entry.

Focus Session Timer

Tap Focus Session to start a 25-minute floating countdown. Finishing it awards +30 XP automatically. Tap the ✕ on the widget to cancel early (no XP awarded).

Final Slay Trial

Unlocks once all Basic tasks are done. It's a timed multiple-choice quiz — pass it to clear the boss's remaining 25% HP, earn the boss's coin/XP reward, and log a slay in your Journal.

AI Import

Copy the Gemini prompt from the AI tab, paste it into an AI chat to generate a custom boss, then paste the returned JSON back in to import a new boss config with fresh tasks and a challenge.

Journal

Tracks your slain bosses, milestone badges, and overall stats over time — a record of consistency, not just a single day's progress.

Shop

Spend Coins (earned from slaying bosses) on cosmetic titles and Battle Avatars — weapon/gear icons shown in your header chip and next to your name on the Rankings leaderboard. Equip a purchased item to display it.

Rankings

Compares you against other players by Weekly XP, Total Slays, or Total Questions (switch sort at the top). Requires an active internet connection and Firebase sign-in.

Profile & Weekly Reset

Tap your name chip in the header to set/change your player handle. Weekly XP automatically resets every Monday — Coins, Slays, and Journal history are permanent and never reset.
